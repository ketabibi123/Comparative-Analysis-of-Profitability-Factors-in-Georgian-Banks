# Comparative Analysis of Profitability Factors in Georgian Banks

A quantitative analysis of the profitability of major Georgian commercial banks compared with industry averages over **2018–2024**.

The study examines **Bank of Georgia, TBC Bank, BasisBank, Credo Bank, and VTB Bank Georgia**, representing approximately 90% of the banking sector's total assets.

## Research Focus

The analysis examines how key financial indicators influenced bank profitability across different economic periods:

* Pre-COVID period: 2018–2019
* COVID-19 shock: 2020
* Recovery: 2021–2022
* Post-2022 period: 2023–2024

## Financial Indicators

The analysis covers:

* Return on Assets (ROA)
* Return on Equity (ROE)
* Net Interest Margin (NIM)
* Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR)
* Loan-to-Asset Ratio
* Non-Performing Loans (NPL)
* Equity Multiplier

Individual bank results were compared with the **industry average** to identify differences in profitability and financial structure.

## Data & Methodology

Financial data was collected from publicly available bank reports and industry sources.

The analysis includes:

* Financial ratio calculation
* Industry benchmarking
* Comparative analysis between banks
* Trend analysis across 2018–2024
* Correlation analysis
* Interpretation of changes across different economic periods

## Key Findings

The analysis identified significant differences between banks in profitability, operating efficiency, asset structure, and financial leverage.

The results also showed that profitability factors changed considerably during the COVID-19 period and subsequent recovery, highlighting the importance of considering broader economic conditions when evaluating bank performance.
